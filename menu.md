## 01 — Moroccan Heritage (التراث المغربي)

**The Heart of Dar Al Kadi (قلب دار القاضي)**

This section captures the rich culinary soul of Morocco, where slow-cooking traditions, aromatic spices, and time-honored hospitality take center stage.

### Zaalouk (زعلوك)

A traditional Moroccan cooked salad with a rich, smoky character.

**Ingredients:** Roasted eggplant, tomato, garlic, cumin, paprika, fresh herbs, and extra-virgin olive oil.

### Fassi Chicken Tagine (طاجين الدجاج الفاسي)

A classic expression of Moroccan hospitality balancing brightness and depth.

**Ingredients:** Halal chicken marinated in chermoula, saffron, garlic, ginger, cilantro, and parsley, slowly cooked with preserved lemon and Moroccan olives.

### Fassi Lamb Tagine (طاجين اللحم الفاسي)

Inspired by the refined sweet-and-savory tradition of Fassi cuisine.

**Ingredients:** Tender halal lamb slowly braised with saffron, ginger, cinnamon, and Moroccan spices, finished with honey-glazed prunes, toasted sesame, and almonds.

### Fassi Beef Tagine (طاجين اللحم البقري الفاسي)

A comforting celebration of Morocco's love for slow cooking and generous flavors.

**Ingredients:** Tender halal beef slowly braised with aromatic Moroccan spices and caramelized onions, finished with sweet prunes and toasted almonds.

## 02 — Mediterranean Table (المائدة المتوسطية)

**Freshness from the Mediterranean (نضارة البحر الأبيض المتوسط)**

Celebrating the vibrant, sun-drenched produce and shared culinary threads connecting Morocco to the broader Mediterranean coast.

### Roasted Mediterranean Vegetables (خضار متوسطية مشوية)

Simple ingredients slowly roasted to bring out their natural sweetness.

**Ingredients:** Eggplant, zucchini, peppers, and tomatoes roasted with garlic, fresh herbs, and extra-virgin olive oil.

### Moroccan & Mediterranean Olives (زيتون مغربي ومتوسطي)

A tribute to the olive tree as a great link between Morocco and the Mediterranean.

**Ingredients:** A selection of Moroccan olives marinated with citrus, herbs, and aromatic spices.

### Hummus (حمص)

A creamy, timeless favorite.

**Ingredients:** Creamy chickpeas, tahini, lemon, garlic, and extra-virgin olive oil, finished with herbs and spices.

### Mediterranean Salads (سلطات متوسطية)

A rotating collection inspired by coastal flavors.

**Ingredients:** Seasonal vegetables, fresh herbs, citrus, and olive oil. (Optional selections include Moroccan carrot & orange, beetroot & citrus, cucumber & mint, tomato & herb, and grilled vegetable salad).

## 03 — Bread & Bakery (الخبز والمعجنات)

**From the Moroccan Oven to the Mediterranean Table (من الفرن المغربي إلى المائدة المتوسطية)**

Bread is sacred at the family table, serving as both an accompaniment and a utensil for sharing tagines and dips.

### Khobz (خبز)

Traditional Moroccan round bread with a rustic crust, ideal for tagines, salads, and sharing.

**Ingredients:** Traditional dough baked into a round loaf with a rustic crust.

### Msemmem (مسمن)

A beloved Moroccan flatbread enjoyed at breakfast, gatherings, and family tables.

**Ingredients:** Semolina and flour, prepared as a flaky, layered flatbread traditionally cooked on a hot griddle.

### Focaccia (فوكاتشيا)

Italian craftsmanship with a Mediterranean spirit.

**Ingredients:** Italian olive-oil bread baked with herbs and sea salt, with optional toppings of olives, cherry tomatoes, rosemary, or roasted peppers.

### Artisan Sourdough (خبز العجين المخمر)

A modern staple for the table.

**Ingredients:** Naturally fermented bread with a crisp crust and deep flavor.

## 04 — Sweets (الحلويات)

**Moroccan Traditions & Mediterranean Indulgence (تقاليد مغربية ومتعة متوسطية)**

An exquisite finale featuring celebratory Moroccan pastries scented with floral waters alongside comforting Mediterranean classics.

### Tiramisu (تيراميسو)

An Italian classic elevated with Dar Al Kadi's signature touches.

**Ingredients:** Espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream, finished with cocoa. (Signature flavor options include Orange Blossom, Date & Orange Blossom, and Pistachio).

### Kaab El Ghazal (كعب الغزال)

A classic pastry associated with Moroccan celebrations and hospitality.

**Ingredients:** Delicate Moroccan pastry filled with almond paste, cinnamon, and orange blossom water.

### M'Hancha (المحنشة)

An impressive, celebratory coiled pastry.

**Ingredients:** Coiled almond pastry scented with orange blossom, finished with honey and toasted pistachios.

### Ghriba d'Louz (غريبة اللوز)

Traditional Moroccan almond cookies.

**Ingredients:** Almonds prepared with a delicate texture and a subtle orange-blossom fragrance.

### Date & Almond Bites (حلوى التمر واللوز)

A simple combination deeply rooted in Moroccan and Mediterranean food culture.

**Ingredients:** Premium dates filled or coated with almonds, sesame, and aromatic spices.

## 05 — Tea & Beverages (الشاي والمشروبات)

**A Moroccan Welcome (ترحيب مغربي)**

Beverages designed to refresh, comfort, and welcome guests in true North African and Mediterranean tradition.

### Moroccan Mint Tea (أتاي بالنعناع)

More than a drink—it is a gesture of welcome.

**Ingredients:** Traditional green tea brewed with fresh mint and served in traditional Moroccan glasses.

### Orange Blossom Tea (شاي زهر البرتقال)

A soothing, floral experience.

**Ingredients:** Delicate aromatic tea infused with orange blossom, served warm.

### Lemon & Mint Cooler (مشروب الليمون والنعناع البارد)

A crisp, revitalizing option.

**Ingredients:** Fresh lemon, mint, and a touch of sweetness, served chilled.

### Seasonal Moroccan-Mediterranean Beverages (مشروبات موسمية)

**Ingredients:** Fresh seasonal fruit, citrus, herbs, and floral flavors.

### Almond & Milk / Lait et Amande (حليب باللوز)

A delicate chilled almond beverage.

**Ingredients:** Almonds, milk, orange blossom water, and a touch of sweetness.
